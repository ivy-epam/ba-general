# EL eBusiness IS Business Operations Analyst - China EasyOrder Program


# Role & Responsibilities

## Role: EasyOrder - Business Engagement Manager (CN)

The role orchestrates the delivery of IS operations activities pertaining to eBusiness Web Shop "EasyOrder" (EO) for China market, ensuring effective coordination of internal stakeholders and external third-party supplier resources to keep the service at the desired level. 

The role hierarchically report to local IT Line Management, and functionally to Global Core EO IT Team (Global IS).

## Why This Role Matters

This role addresses critical challenges in the Global-China coordination:

1. **Communication & Alignment Gaps** — Ineffective communication between global and China teams leading to miscommunication and misalignments on planned tasks and deliverables.

2. **Lack of System Feedback** — Low visibility and feedback on system stability and status of committed change requests, limiting ability to plan and respond effectively.

3. **Slow Response & Turnaround** — Extended response times and slow turnaround on issue resolution, impacting business continuity and customer satisfaction.

4. **Customer Dissatisfaction** — Increased complaints from customers regarding unfriendly new features or changes, reflecting poor UX communication and change management.

5. **Release & Change Transparency** — Inadequate transparency over releases and changes, creating uncertainty and hampering stakeholder confidence.

6. **Trust & Cooperation Issues** — Low trust between global and local teams, ineffective information exchange, and poor collaboration hindering smooth operations.

7. **Unaligned Expectations** — Complaints from local business and unreasonable asks not aligned with global guidelines, creating friction and rework.

This role bridges these gaps by serving as the central point of contact for Chinese stakeholders, ensuring clear communication, expectation management, and effective coordination across all parties.


## Responsibilities

1. **Customer Onboarding Management** — Collaborate on 3-wave onboarding of Chinese customers onto the new EasyOrder platform, ensuring minimal disruption and maximum adoption.

2. **Module Cutover & Migration** — Manage seamless transition of modules from legacy cBOL to new EasyOrder system, coordinating with technical and business stakeholders.

3. **Incident Management** — Manage full lifecycle of incidents reported by Chinese team and local customers, ensuring issues are accurately understood, documented, escalated with context, and resolved within SLAs.

4. **Technical Coordination & Bug Resolution** — Coordinate with development and support teams for bug fixing, issue resolution, and technical troubleshooting. Provide initial troubleshooting and impact analysis to L2 teams, reducing escalation back-and-forth and accelerating resolution timelines.

5. **Change Request Analysis & Coordination** — Analyze and refine new change requests from the Chinese team, assess technical solutions, and facilitate communication between development and business stakeholders to ensure alignment and feasibility.

6. **UAT & Quality Assurance** — Assist in User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and conduct smoke tests on production environments to identify and prevent regression bugs, ensuring system stability and quality.

7. **Stakeholder Reporting** — Prepare and deliver weekly reports to local Chinese business leadership and global business stakeholders on program status, progress metrics, risks, and issues.

- Serve as the primary point of contact for Chinese business stakeholders, understanding their needs, requirements, and expectations across all EasyOrder operations.
