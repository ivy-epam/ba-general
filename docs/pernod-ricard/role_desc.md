# Role Description: China PR Data Portal QA Specialist

## Background

The Data Portal is a web portal available at Pernod Ricard for employees to explore, find, understand, and consume the organization's data in all its forms: access to data, BI reports, and various data-related projects within large companies.

The Data Portal is part of the Data Platform — the technical stack with tools and services, along with every data/resource they contain.

DP has separated China and global infrastructure for regulatory concerns, and the global team has no access to the China system. As a result, a local role is required to help perform functional testing on the stage environment and smoke testing on the production environment, and to serve as the system SME for the local IT teams.

## Why This Role Is Required

Because China and global infrastructure are separated for regulatory reasons, the global team has no visibility or access into the China system. This leaves a gap in local QA coverage, local system expertise, and a point of contact for the China IT team — without a dedicated local role, there would be no one able to validate weekly rollouts on stage, verify production health via smoke testing, or answer system questions on the ground. This role fills that gap, acting as the local QA owner and SME so the China Data Portal can be tested, supported, and launched without depending on the global team's direct access.

## Role

QA expert for the China PR Data Portal. Managed by the global DP project team.

## Responsibilities

- Creating test cases and conducting functional testing and smoke testing for weekly rollouts
- Assisting in and preparing for the official launch of the platform in China
- Serving as the system SME for the local IT teams, managing queries and handling requests
- Managing product incidents and the bugfixing lifecycle to ensure smooth delivery of fixes

## Achievements

- Helped drive the successful launch of the platform in China
- Conducted high-quality testing to ensure system quality and user satisfaction
- Maintained a low number of user complaints and incidents
- Received recognition from the product owner for the contribution and improvement brought to system quality
- Identified issues not only in the China environment but also in the global environment
