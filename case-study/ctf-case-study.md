## 3. Industry & Business Context

FTLife (formerly CTF Life) is a leading life insurance provider in Hong Kong and Macau, pursuing a strategic push into mainland China's rapidly growing life insurance market — a sector shaped by tight regulatory oversight, a traditionally agent-led distribution model, and intensifying competition as more insurers chase mainland growth. 

## 4. Problem Statement

FTLife's agent recruitment process was paper-based and manual, creating a slow, opaque pipeline just as the company needed to scale aggressively into a new market.

- **Narrow prospecting pipeline**: paper-based processes limited the reach and visibility of the lead/prospect pipeline, constraining growth right when FTLife needed to scale rapidly.
- **Isolated onboarding processes**: different teams used disconnected tools and platforms for onboarding, creating inconsistent, siloed handoffs.
- **Long review and contracting lead times**: manual review, approval, and agent-code creation steps caused long lead times and candidate drop-off before onboarding completed.
- **Limited transparency and reporting**: without a centralized dashboard, it was hard to track conversion, turnaround time, or agent quality across the pipeline.

## 5. Solution Delivered

| Feature / Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Multi-persona login & RBAC | Role-based access tailored to prospects, hiring managers, recruiters, and operations reviewers, each with a dedicated view of the workflow. |
| Digital prospect & application management | Online application capture in the prospect's view, with centralized prospect/application management for hiring managers and event/activity organization for recruiters. |
| Automated review & approval workflow | Streamlined reviewing and approving workflow for operations, replacing manual, paper-based sign-off. |
| Contracting & e-signature | Contract generation/uploading and e-signature to close out onboarding digitally. |
| Analytics & reporting pipeline | ETL pipeline feeding a Power BI dashboard, surfacing recruitment performance and reporting across the funnel. |
| Event-triggered notifications & SLA | Automated notifications tied to workflow events and service-level targets to keep the pipeline moving. |
| Phased delivery (MVP1 → MVP2 → Phase 1) | Delivered in phases — core recruitment activities/events (MVP1), the complete recruitment journey (MVP2), and wider system integration (Phase 1). |

## 6. My Role & Key Contributions

**Title:** BA Lead.

Brought in to reshape BA workflow and practices for this greenfield digital recruitment platform, while owning user manual creation and stakeholder engagement. As BA Lead, I:

- **Standardized BA practices** — helped standardize BA practices for the greenfield project, and produced requirement documentation and user manuals to support adoption by operations and management teams.
- **Coordinated cross-team alignment** — aligned the PO, business stakeholders, onshore BA, and engineering team on automation scope, analytics requirements, and phased delivery.
- **Assessed the as-is process** — assessed the existing manual recruitment process, documenting pain points in candidate management, agent onboarding, and reporting to define the automation scope.
- **Elicited and validated requirements** — covered automated recruitment workflows, including eligibility rules, approval routing, role-based access, regulatory data retention, and performance reporting.
- **Facilitated backlog grooming and solution design** — for workflow features, the ETL pipeline, and Power BI dashboards, ensuring technical solutions met business and reporting needs.
