## 3. Industry & Business Context

FTLife (formerly CTF Life) is a leading life insurance provider in Hong Kong and Macau, pursuing a strategic push into mainland China's rapidly growing life insurance market — a sector shaped by tight regulatory oversight, a traditionally agent-led distribution model, and intensifying competition as more insurers chase mainland growth. 

## 4. Problem Statement

FTLife was replacing its paper-based, manual agent recruitment process — which suffered from narrow prospecting reach, siloed onboarding tools, long review and contracting lead times, and little pipeline reporting — with a digital recruitment platform. However, delivery of the new platform was held back by several issues:

- **Low end-user readiness**: end users struggled to understand how to use the new system because proper documentation and guidelines were missing.
- **Ineffective BA workflow**: the workflow between onshore business and offshore BAs was ineffective, delaying requirement analysis, confirmation, and sign-off, and leading to frequent requirement changes.
- **Ineffective business–dev communication**: many details went unverified or were specified late, causing delays and issues in implementation.

## 5. Solution Delivered

*Scope note: the solution was to onboard an Offshore BA Lead to improve BA workflows and stakeholder engagement, and to lead the creation of the user manual and guidelines — not to rebuild the recruitment platform itself, which was delivered in phases (MVP1 → MVP2 → Phase 1) by the wider project team.*

| Feature / Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| BA workflow improvement | Reworked the workflow between onshore business and offshore BAs to speed up requirement analysis, confirmation, and sign-off, and to reduce requirement changes. |
| Stakeholder engagement | Bridged business stakeholders, the PO, and the dev team so requirement details were verified and specified early enough for implementation. |
| User manual & operation guidelines | Led the creation of the user manual and usage guidelines, giving end users clear instructions for adopting the new recruitment system. |

## 6. My Role & Responsibilities
