## 3. Industry & Business Context

Pernod Ricard is one of the world's largest spirits and wine companies, operating across highly regulated, geographically diverse markets. As a multinational consumer goods player, the company relies on an enterprise-wide Data Portal to let employees explore and consume BI reports and data assets — but China's data residency and cross-border data regulations force this platform to run as a separate, isolated instance there. 

## 4. Problem Statement

Because China's infrastructure for the Data Portal was walled off from the global system for regulatory reasons, the global team had no visibility or access into the China environment — leaving a critical platform launch without local quality assurance or system expertise on the ground.

- **No local QA coverage**: weekly stage rollouts and production releases had no one validating functionality or verifying production health in-region.
- **No local SME**: China IT teams lacked a dedicated point of contact for system questions, requests, and escalations.
- **Launch risk**: the platform's official China launch was at risk without dedicated local testing and support ahead of go-live.

## 5. Solution Delivered

| Feature / Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Functional & smoke test suite | Designed test cases and executed functional testing on stage and smoke testing on production for weekly Data Portal rollouts in China. |
| Launch readiness testing | Planned and executed testing activities supporting the platform's official China launch. |
| Local SME function | Served as the on-the-ground subject matter expert for China IT teams, handling queries and requests. |
| Incident & bugfix lifecycle management | Owned product incident triage through resolution to keep fixes flowing smoothly. |
| Cross-environment defect detection | Identified and reported defects spanning both the China and global environments. |

## 6. My Role & Key Contributions

As China PR Data Portal QA Specialist, I owned quality assurance and local system expertise for a platform the global team couldn't directly access. My responsibilities spanned test case design and functional/smoke testing for weekly rollouts, launch readiness preparation for the official China go-live, acting as the local SME for China IT teams, and managing the end-to-end incident and bugfixing lifecycle. These contributions closed the regulatory-driven gap in local QA coverage, giving stakeholders confidence in weekly releases and production health, and supported a smooth, successful platform launch in China.
