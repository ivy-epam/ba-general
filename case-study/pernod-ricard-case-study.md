## 3. Industry & Business Context

Pernod Ricard is one of the world's largest spirits and wine companies, operating across highly regulated, geographically diverse markets. As a multinational consumer goods player, the company relies on an enterprise-wide Data Portal to let employees explore and consume BI reports and data assets — but China's data residency and cross-border data regulations force this platform to run as a separate, isolated instance there. 

## 4. Problem Statement

Because China's infrastructure for the Data Portal was walled off from the global system for regulatory reasons, the global team had no visibility or access into the China environment — leaving a critical platform launch without local quality assurance or system expertise on the ground.

- **No local QA coverage**: no one in-region validated functionality on weekly stage rollouts or verified production health after releases.
- **No local SME**: China IT teams had no dedicated point of contact for system questions, requests, and escalations.
- **Launch risk**: without dedicated local testing and support, the platform's official China launch was at risk.

**Observed issues:**

- **Issues slipping into China environments**: issues went unspotted before release to the China environments, lowering confidence in production rollouts and delaying the schedule.
- **Ineffective global–local communication**: the global and local teams lacked an effective channel for communicating with each other.

## 5. Solution Delivered

*Scope note: the solution was to onboard a dedicated local QA Specialist in China to provide the in-region quality assurance and system expertise the global team couldn't — not to rebuild the Data Portal itself, which remained owned by the global team.*

| Feature / Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Local functional & smoke testing | In-region test case design, functional testing on stage, and smoke testing on production for each weekly Data Portal rollout in China. |
| Launch readiness testing | Planning and executing the testing activities needed for the platform's official China launch. |
| Local SME function | An on-the-ground subject matter expert for China IT teams, handling system questions and requests. |
| Incident & bugfix lifecycle management | Ownership of China incidents from triage through resolution, keeping fixes flowing to the China environments. |
| Cross-environment defect detection | Identifying and reporting defects in both the China and global environments. |

## 6. My Role & Responsibilities
