## 3. Industry & Business Context

DBS Private Bank, encompassing both Private Bank and Treasures Private Client, serves affluent and high-net-worth individual (HNWI) segments across Singapore and Hong Kong in a private banking and wealth management industry where relationship managers (RMs) are expected to deliver highly personalized, consultative advice to clients with complex and diverse investment and wealth needs. Competitive differentiation increasingly hinges on how quickly and holistically RMs can understand a client's full wealth picture and coordinate across specialist teams to act on it.

## 4. Problem Statement

Manual, decentralized account-planning processes left DBS's relationship managers with less time for the deep client analysis their HNWI clients needed.

- **Manual handover processes**: account plans changed hands manually between RMs and teams, creating administrative burden and risk of lost context.
- **Decentralized client data**: HNWI client and wealth data was scattered across systems rather than held in a single, unified view.
- **Segmented procedures across teams**: front office, executives, and specialist teams worked from disconnected processes, limiting joint client engagement.
- **Less time for high-value advice**: the administrative burden of maintaining account plans left RMs with less time for in-depth HNWI analysis, hindering personalized advice and service delivery.

## 5. Solution Delivered

| Feature / Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Digitalized Account Plan journey | Launched a digitalized Account Plan journey onboarded to the bank's CRM system, integrated with diverse subsystems and data sources. |
| Unified 360-degree HNWI view | Gave Front Office RMs a unified 360-degree view of HNWIs, seamlessly integrated with wealth and account data. |
| Centralized Account Plan hub | Centralized hub for viewing Account Plans, with real-time updates and tracking of client strategies. |
| Centralized monitoring for Executives & Managers | Gave executives and managers a single source of controlling and monitoring, enabling efficient joint client engagement across teams and accessibility for specialist teams. |
| Excel-to-system migration | Migrated Excel-based account plan records into the new Account Plan system. |
| Subsystem connectivity | Established connectivity with subsystems for financials and static data, enriching the client and wealth data repository and strengthening opportunity identification and prompting capabilities. |

## 6. My Role & Key Contributions

**Title:** Business Analyst.

As BA on this Account Plan digitalization initiative, I:

- **Mapped end-to-end user journeys and built a prioritized feature backlog** — covering RM account-planning workflows, working with the Wealth Management team.
- **Specified data models and integration points** — across core banking systems (Finacle, Avaloq, CRM) to create a single source of truth for client data and portfolio holdings feeding the Account Plan.
- **Elicited and documented account-planning requirements** — working with compliance and risk stakeholders to translate regulatory rules into specifications and acceptance criteria.
- **Authored user stories with the Product Owner** — prioritized the backlog and coordinated dev teams in Singapore and China through implementation, managing dependencies with the Avaloq and Finacle workstreams.
- **Supported the Architect and Tech Lead** — on microservice design and database schemas, and worked with UI/UX designers on Figma wireframes.
- **Ran desk checks and pre-UAT testing** — and produced functional knowledge specs, user manuals, and training to support other teams, leadership, and operations.
