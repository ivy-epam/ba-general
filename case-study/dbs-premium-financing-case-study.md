## 3. Industry & Business Context

DBS is a multinational banking and financial services corporation with a strong strategic presence in Hong Kong within the APEC framework, built around a sizeable lending business. Premium financing — enabled by growing synergy between financial intermediaries and insurers — has emerged as a promising new revenue stream, making faster, more reliable loan processing a competitive priority.

## 4. Problem Statement

DBS's premium financing loan workflow — a fast-growing revenue stream for the bank — ran on lengthy, offline handovers that were causing delays and threatening the business's market competitiveness.

- **Lengthy handovers**: loan applications passed manually between departments, extending the overall processing timeline.
- **Offline processes**: much of the workflow relied on manual, paperwork-based handling rather than automated systems.
- **Applicant frustration**: slow turnaround times frustrated applicants waiting on loan decisions.
- **Competitive risk**: the delays put the bank's market competitiveness in premium financing at risk, just as the segment was becoming a significant new revenue stream.

## 5. Solution Delivered

*Scope note: as the Business Analyst, I owned requirement engineering and validation for the capabilities below, assisted the Product Owner with the backlog, and collaborated with the Product Manager on feature delivery and releases.*

| Feature / Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Digital application initiation | Enabled RMs to initiate premium financing loan applications digitally, replacing paper-based starts. |
| Automated KYC & risk/credit checks | Automated KYC and risk/credit checks within the application flow, with business and calculation logic embedded directly into the workflow. |
| Real-time progress tracking & dashboard | Centralized dataflow and a dashboard gave real-time visibility into application progress, with prompt updates and notifications. |
| Automated inter-team data/task transfer | Automated data and task handoffs between teams, with intelligent routing and workflow triggers replacing manual transfers. |
| Centralized application view & access control | Centralized view of application details, embedded risk assessment/deviation handling, and access control by team and role. |
| Event-triggered loan processing | Automated drawdown and transfer activation, with error handling, transaction failure contingency, and a procedure monitoring state machine. |

## 6. My Role & Responsibilities

**Title:** Business Analyst

I was the BA on this premium financing loan automation initiative, focusing on requirement engineering and validation and assisting the Product Owner. I worked with the Wealth Management team, compliance and risk stakeholders, and cross-location dev squads in Singapore and China.

- **Requirements elicitation and specification** — mapping end-to-end user journeys for digital onboarding, RM workflows, and compliance-gated loan processes with the Wealth Management team, and eliciting KYC and premium-financing requirements (identity verification, CDD/EDD, credit risk screening), translating regulatory rules into specifications and acceptance criteria with compliance and risk stakeholders.
- **Data and integration specification** — specifying data models and integration points across core banking systems (Finacle, Avaloq, CRM) to create a single source of truth for client data, KYC status, and portfolio holdings, and owning API/system analysis, table design, and data mapping.
- **Requirements validation** — running desk checks and pre-UAT testing on KYC and compliance features to validate delivered functionality against the agreed requirements and acceptance criteria.
- **Product Owner assistance** — authoring user stories and use cases with the Product Owner, and building and prioritizing the feature backlog.
- **Delivery and release collaboration** — collaborating with the Product Manager on feature delivery and releases, hosting product discovery, kick-off, sprint ceremonies, and client demos via Jira/Confluence, coordinating dev squads in Singapore and China through implementation, and managing dependencies with the Avaloq and Finacle workstreams.
- **Solution design support** — supporting the Architect and Tech Lead on microservice design and database schemas for KYC records, audit trails, and compliance reporting, and UI/UX designers on Figma wireframes.
- **Knowledge transfer and enablement** — producing functional knowledge specs, user manuals, and training for other teams, leadership, and operations.
