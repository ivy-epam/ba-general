## 3. Industry & Business Context

DBS is a multinational banking and financial services corporation with a strong strategic presence in Hong Kong within the APEC framework, built around a sizeable lending business. Premium financing — enabled by growing synergy between financial intermediaries and insurers — has emerged as a promising new revenue stream, making faster, more reliable loan processing a competitive priority.

## 4. Problem Statement

DBS's premium financing loan workflow — a fast-growing revenue stream for the bank — ran on lengthy, offline handovers that were causing delays and threatening the business's market competitiveness.

- **Lengthy handovers**: loan applications passed manually between departments, extending the overall processing timeline.
- **Offline processes**: much of the workflow relied on manual, paperwork-based handling rather than automated systems.
- **Applicant frustration**: slow turnaround times frustrated applicants waiting on loan decisions.
- **Competitive risk**: the delays put the bank's market competitiveness in premium financing at risk, just as the segment was becoming a significant new revenue stream.

## 5. Solution Delivered

| Feature / Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Digital application initiation | Enabled RMs to initiate premium financing loan applications digitally, replacing paper-based starts. |
| Automated KYC & risk/credit checks | Automated KYC and risk/credit checks within the application flow, with business and calculation logic embedded directly into the workflow. |
| Real-time progress tracking & dashboard | Centralized dataflow and a dashboard gave real-time visibility into application progress, with prompt updates and notifications. |
| Automated inter-team data/task transfer | Automated data and task handoffs between teams, with intelligent routing and workflow triggers replacing manual transfers. |
| Centralized application view & access control | Centralized view of application details, embedded risk assessment/deviation handling, and access control by team and role. |
| Event-triggered loan processing | Automated drawdown and transfer activation, with error handling, transaction failure contingency, and a procedure monitoring state machine. |

## 6. My Role & Key Contributions

**Title:** Business Analyst.

As BA on this premium financing loan automation initiative, I:

- **Mapped end-to-end user journeys and built a prioritized feature backlog** — covering digital onboarding, RM workflows, and compliance-gated processes for the loan journey, working with the Wealth Management team.
- **Specified data models and integration points** — across core banking systems (Finacle, Avaloq, CRM) to create a single source of truth for client data, KYC status, and portfolio holdings.
- **Elicited and documented KYC and premium-financing requirements** — covering identity verification, CDD/EDD, and credit risk screening, working with compliance and risk stakeholders to translate regulatory rules into specifications and acceptance criteria.
- **Authored user stories with the Product Owner** — prioritized the backlog and coordinated dev teams in Singapore and China through implementation, managing dependencies with the Avaloq and Finacle workstreams.
- **Supported the Architect and Tech Lead** — on microservice design and database schemas for KYC records, audit trails, and compliance reporting, and worked with UI/UX designers on Figma wireframes.
- **Ran desk checks and pre-UAT testing** — on KYC and compliance features, and produced functional knowledge specs, user manuals, and training to support other teams, leadership, and operations.
- **Ran agile BA ceremonies and artifacts** — hosted product discovery, kick-off, sprint ceremonies, and client demos via Jira/Confluence, and owned use case analysis, the requirements backlog, API/system analysis, table design and data mapping, and solution design across cross-location dev squads.
